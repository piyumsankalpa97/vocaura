# Vocaura Design Direction

## Brand

**Vocaura**

**Positioning:** A calm, modern space for improving your professional voice.

**Tone:** thoughtful, confident, human, focused.

Avoid anything that looks like a generic AI SaaS, children's language app, or overly corporate HR platform.

## Color Direction

### Primary palette

- **Deep Forest:** `#173F35`
- **Soft Sage:** `#A8B8A5`
- **Warm Ivory:** `#F7F4EC`
- **Ink:** `#202522`
- **Muted Stone:** `#777C76`
- **White:** `#FFFFFF`

Use Deep Forest as the main brand color rather than the typical AI indigo/purple.

Use Soft Sage and Warm Ivory for supporting surfaces and highlights.

Keep the interface mostly neutral, with green appearing intentionally rather than everywhere.

### Optional accent

**Terracotta:** `#C9795D`

Use sparingly for small highlights, milestones, or moments that need warmth.

## Typography

**Primary:** Inter

Clean, highly readable, and suitable for both dashboards and long-form feedback.

Use strong but restrained typography. Avoid oversized SaaS-style headlines.

## Visual Style

- Minimal
- Editorial
- Calm
- Slightly warm
- Data-informed without feeling clinical
- Plenty of whitespace
- Subtle borders
- Soft corner radius
- Light shadows only when useful
- Avoid excessive gradients
- Avoid glassmorphism
- Avoid neon colors
- Avoid excessive rounded cards
- Avoid generic AI visual language

## Dashboard Feel

The dashboard should feel closer to a thoughtful productivity tool than a language-learning game.

Focus visually on:

- Current progress
- Recent practice
- Personal weaknesses
- Today's challenge
- Improvement over time
- Useful insights

Progress should feel encouraging without becoming childish or gamified.

## Data Visualization

Keep charts simple and restrained.

Prefer:

- Line charts for improvement over time
- Small trend indicators
- Circular/radial progress for major skill areas
- Simple comparison views
- Clear numerical scores

Avoid dashboard clutter.

## Photography / Illustration

If visuals are needed, prefer:

- Real human photography
- Natural environments
- Subtle editorial imagery
- Abstract speech/audio textures

Avoid:

- Cartoon characters
- Robot illustrations
- AI brains
- Glowing neural networks
- Stock-photo business handshakes

## Brand Mark Direction

Explore a simple **V** based on:

- Speech
- Voice waves
- Conversation
- Flow
- Two people communicating

Keep it abstract and minimal.

The wordmark should work well on its own without requiring a large logo.

## Overall Reference

Think:

**Notion + Linear + a calm editorial language-learning product**

rather than:

**Duolingo + generic AI dashboard**

## Core Principle

The interface should make the user feel:

> "I'm getting better at expressing myself."

Not:

> "I'm using an AI tool."
